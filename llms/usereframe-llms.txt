# Reframe

> Reframe (usereframe.ai) is a Y Combinator Winter 2026 company building an AI-native hardware procurement platform. AI agents automate purchase orders, supplier follow-ups, and part tracking across hardware supply chains, for both COTS and custom-manufactured components, with Slack and Gmail integrations. Reframe does not currently publish a public developer API.

Founded in 2025 in San Francisco by Bryan Zin (previously Product Design Engineer, iPhone, at Apple) and Eric Wiener.
